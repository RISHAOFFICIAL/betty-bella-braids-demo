About Betty

Betty Bella Braids is a human hair specialist and the name behind one of the most followed braiding pages in the country, with 180,000 followers and a growing YouTube channel. From her physical shop in Kalamazoo, Michigan, Betty serves clients across Detroit and Portage with styles that look natural, feel light, and last.

Every set is tailored to the client. Real human hair, clean parts, and a finish that turns heads.

“Your hair is your crown. I treat every set like it belongs on a runway.”

Betty, Founder
